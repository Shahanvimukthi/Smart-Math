LESSON 01

Integers: numbers on a line

Integers include positive numbers, negative numbers, and zero. Think of a lift: floors above ground are positive, and basement floors are negative.

QUICK EXAMPLE−3 < 0 < 4Numbers farther to the right on a number line are greater.
LESSON 02

Fractions: parts of a whole

A fraction shows how many equal parts we have. The top number is the numerator; the bottom number is the denominator.

QUICK EXAMPLE¼ + ¼ = ½One quarter and one quarter make one half.
LESSON 03

Algebra: letters can be numbers

A letter such as x stands for a number we do not know yet. Use the same operation on both sides to find it.

QUICK EXAMPLEx + 3 = 8   →   x = 5Subtract 3 from both sides to find x.
